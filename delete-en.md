# Delete your Nuntteum account and data

How to delete your account and the data stored on our servers for Nuntteum (developer: Hyun Taek Oh).

## Delete in the app
1. Open Nuntteum.
2. Tap **Alarms tab > Settings (gear, top right) > "Delete account"** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete** in the confirmation. Everything is deleted right away.

## Request without the app
If you uninstalled the app or can't open it, email **mylvoh0714@gmail.com** to request deletion.
- Include the Google account email you linked, or the account ID (8 characters) shown in Settings.
- We delete your data within 7 days of the request and reply to you.
- If you uninstalled the app without linking a Google account, we can't identify your account and can't delete it.

## What is deleted
- Your account (anonymous account ID, and the linked Google account email and ID)
- Server backups (alarm settings, progress records, planner and notes)
- Couple pairing, morning-call recordings, nudges and wake-up records
- Goals with friends: your check-ins, check-in notes and photos, cheers and nudges, reports and blocks. A group you are the last member of is deleted entirely.
- Subscription records and daily usage counts

All of it is deleted **immediately** and not kept anywhere.

## What is not deleted
- Alarms, planner and voices on your phone are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.** Cancel it in Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions. Google keeps payment records under its own policies.

See the [Privacy Policy](en.html) for details.
